# Iron Man Night Flight: 5-Minute Script

Target runtime: about 5 minutes
Recommended structure: 30 clips x about 10 seconds each
Generation note: render in batches and stitch later for reliability on HPC

## Story Beats

1. Iron Man stands on a rain-swept skyscraper at dusk, looking over Manhattan.
2. Close-up on the arc reactor pulsing through the armor as thunder rolls.
3. He launches into the night sky, repulsors igniting beneath the storm clouds.
4. The camera tracks beside him as he cuts through neon-lit towers.
5. A warning interface blooms across the HUD: unknown drones detected near the river.
6. He banks hard over the water, city reflections breaking beneath him.
7. A swarm of hostile drones rises from barges hidden in the fog.
8. Iron Man dodges the first missile volley in a burst of sparks and vapor.
9. He lands on a bridge span to shield civilians trapped in stalled traffic.
10. Repulsor fire erupts as he intercepts incoming drones above the roadway.
11. He saves a family from a collapsing section of the bridge.
12. A damaged drone transmits a strange corporate insignia before self-destructing.
13. Back at the tower workshop, Tony Stark studies fragments on a holographic bench.
14. He realizes the drones are powered by a stolen arc-core derivative.
15. Mechanical arms descend to fit upgraded shoulder armor and new thrusters.
16. Tony tests the revised suit in the lab with brighter, tighter repulsor beams.
17. He traces the signal to an abandoned coastal energy plant outside the city.
18. Iron Man rockets across the shoreline as dawn blue creeps into the sky.
19. He infiltrates the ruined facility, entering through shattered steel vents.
20. Inside, dormant assembly lines begin waking up around him.
21. A massive command drone emerges from the darkness, larger than the rest.
22. Iron Man fights through heavy machine fire in a corridor of sparks and smoke.
23. He is knocked through a reactor chamber wall and nearly loses power.
24. Tony reroutes the arc reactor from life support to weapons for one final push.
25. The suit powers up with a brighter blue core and overcharged repulsors.
26. He blasts through a wave of drones and reaches the central control tower.
27. The command drone locks the plant into meltdown to stop him.
28. Iron Man rips open the core shielding and redirects the overload skyward.
29. A pillar of blue-white energy tears through the clouds as the plant collapses safely inward.
30. At sunrise, Iron Man hovers above the quiet skyline, battered but victorious.

## Tone

- Cinematic superhero action
- Photoreal metallic armor
- Rain, fog, reflections, sparks, and blue-white energy
- Heroic but slightly melancholic atmosphere

## Production Advice

- Render in 5-clip or 10-clip batches instead of all 30 at once.
- Keep `continue_from_previous` enabled to preserve visual continuity.
- Expect a full 5-minute assembly to take substantial GPU time and storage.
